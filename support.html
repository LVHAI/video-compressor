Frequently Asked Questions

How does VideosSlim compress videos?

VideosSlim uses advanced encoding technology to reduce video file size while preserving visual quality. You can choose from presets (Light, Standard, Max) or customize resolution, bitrate, frame rate, and codec manually.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. All video processing happens entirely on your device. Your videos never leave your iPhone or iPad. VideosSlim does not require an internet connection and does not collect any personal data.

Where do compressed videos get saved?

Compressed videos are saved directly to your Photos album. You can find them alongside your other videos in the Photos app.

Why is the compressed video sometimes larger than the original?

Videos that have already been heavily compressed may not benefit from further compression. In these cases, VideosSlim automatically keeps the original file to avoid making it larger.

How many videos can I compress at once?

Free users can compress 1 video per day. Paid users can batch compress up to 5 videos at once with unlimited daily usage.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchase". Your purchase will be restored automatically if you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

Does VideosSlim work offline?

Yes. VideosSlim works entirely offline. No internet connection is required for compression.

Which video formats are supported?

VideosSlim supports all common video formats including MP4, MOV, and HEVC. Output format is MP4 with H.264 or H.265 codec.

Can I cancel a compression in progress?

Yes. Tap the X button at the top-left of the progress screen to cancel. Any videos already compressed will be saved.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
