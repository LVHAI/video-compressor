Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

🔒 VideoSlim processes all videos entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to VideoSlim ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application VideoSlim (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

VideoSlim does not collect, store, upload, or share any of the following:

  • Your personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Your videos, photos, or any media files
  • Your location data
  • Your device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Your usage patterns or analytics

All video processing happens entirely on your device. Your videos never leave your iPhone or iPad.

3. Permissions We Request

VideoSlim requests the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Photo Library Access: Used to read the videos you select for compression and to save compressed videos back to your album. We do not access videos you have not selected.
  • Notifications (optional): Used to notify you when background compression completes. You can disable this at any time in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

VideoSlim does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

The App uses Apple's In-App Purchase system for the one-time purchase. Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage

All data related to the App (compression settings, statistics, and preferences) is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage. When you uninstall the App, this data is automatically removed by the system.

6. Children's Privacy

VideoSlim does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal information from any user, this is inherently satisfied.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data for you to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact us.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

support@videosslim.app
